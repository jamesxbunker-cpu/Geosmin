The Story

Est. 2026 Independent Canada

This site was created with the name Geosmin, I have always loved the rain and mornings after a night of it always left a pleasant smell. This is named in honor of that. The logo is a testement to a concept I really like, follies. The heart of the concept, in my interpretation, is creating something grandious with more effort put into its appearance than required. I would like to incorperate this into some of my future projects by of course having them work practically, but adding over the top detail to make them stand alone in a timeless fashion.

This site honors other projects and components that are technically adept. This features an open source project and highlights its use and well done details. This is a great learning step for me to directly reflect from others success and what makes them great. I would like to learn PCB design and an early step is getting familiar with the components and design techniques that will be used. The monthly section highliughts commonly used components and PCB design techniques that are best practices and why.
