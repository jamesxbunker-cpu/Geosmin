Welcome to Geosmin

There's a word for the smell of freshly turned earth — geosmin — the scent that lingers after rain.

This site is named after it because there is a beauty to what follows the rain: things that grew quietly, things that surfaced slowly, things that were always there but only now noticed.

Projects, notes, and small experiments by James Bunker, kept here so they don't get lost.
